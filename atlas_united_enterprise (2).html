What will happen:
  • All invoices, payments, returns, expenses and journal entries will be deleted
  • Customer & supplier closing balances will become new opening balances
  • Current stock positions will become new opening stock
  • Invoice counter resets to 001 with new term prefix (e.g. T2-INV-001)
  • All master data (customers, suppliers, products, settings) is preserved
  • A full backup downloads automatically before closing
